You want to type with your eyes on the page. Good. We start with the hands.
Find F and J. Those two keys have a small bump. That bump is home.
Close your eyes. Index fingers only. Find the bumps. Don't peek.
Left index stays on F. Right index stays on J. That is the home row.
Curve the other fingers onto A S D and K L semicolon. Thumbs rest on space.
To type U, the right index steps up one key, then walks straight back to J.
One rule. Never look down at the keys while you type.
The bumps bring you home. The screen holds your eyes.
After a few days the hands move and the mind stays on the words.
Ready. First drill is the home row. Let's go.
