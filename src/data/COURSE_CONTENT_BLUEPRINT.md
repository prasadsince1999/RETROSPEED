## 1. Core Touch Typing & Keyboarding Foundations

### Course: `retrospeed-odyssey` (Primary Path · 97 Lessons)
* **Stage 1: The Home Row (23 Lessons · Goal: 15 WPM)**
  * Lesson 1: `Home Row Desk & Tactile Bumps` (type: `motion`, keys: `f, j, u, space`)
  * Lesson 2: `Keys F & J` (type: `intro`, keys: `f, j`)
  * Lesson 3: `The Spacebar` (type: `practice`, keys: `space, f, j`)
  * Lesson 4: `F, J & Space` (type: `review`, keys: `f, j, space`)
  * Lesson 5: `Keys D & K` (type: `intro`, keys: `d, k`)
  * Lesson 6: `D, K + F, J` (type: `review`, keys: `d, k, f, j, space`)
  * Lesson 7: `D & K (Timed Drill)` (type: `drill`, keys: `d, k, f, j, space`)
  * Lesson 8: `Press Room: Home Four` (type: `game`, gameId: `press-room`, keys: `f, j, k, d, space`)
  * Lesson 9: `Keys S & L` (type: `intro`, keys: `s, l`)
  * Lesson 10: `S, L + Known Keys` (type: `review`, keys: `s, l, d, k, f, j, space`)
  * Lesson 11: `S, L (Timed Drill)` (type: `drill`, keys: `s, l, d, k, f, j, space`)
  * Lesson 12: `Keys A & ;` (type: `intro`, keys: `a, ;`)
  * Lesson 13: `A, ; + Known Keys` (type: `review`, keys: `a, ;, s, l, d, k, f, j, space`)
  * Lesson 14: `First Eight Keys Drill` (type: `drill`, keys: `a, s, d, f, j, k, l, ;, space`)
  * Lesson 15: `Press Room: Home Eight` (type: `game`, gameId: `press-room`, keys: `a, s, d, f, j, k, l, ;, space`)
  * Lesson 16: `Coach: Eyes on Page` (type: `motion`)
  * Lesson 17: `Left Hand Anchor (ASDF)` (type: `anchor`, hand: `left`)
  * Lesson 18: `Right Hand Anchor (JKL;)` (type: `anchor`, hand: `right`)
  * Lesson 19: `Keys G & H` (type: `intro`, keys: `g, h`)
  * Lesson 20: `Full Home Row Review` (type: `review`, keys: `a, s, d, f, g, h, j, k, l, ;, space`)
  * Lesson 21: `Full Home Row Speed Drill` (type: `drill`, keys: `a, s, d, f, g, h, j, k, l, ;, space`)
  * Lesson 22: `Checkpoint: Home Row Master` (type: `checkpoint`)
  * Lesson 23: `Play: Home Row Boss` (type: `game`, gameId: `paper-planes`)
* **Stage 2: The Top Row (16 Lessons · Goal: 20 WPM)**
  * Lessons 24–39: Pairs E & I, R & U, T & Y, W & O, Q & P, Top Row Reviews, and Arcade Games.
* **Stage 3: The Bottom Row (14 Lessons · Goal: 22 WPM)**
  * Lessons 40–53: Pairs V & M, C & Comma, X & Period, Z & Slash, and Bottom Row Boss.
* **Stage 4: Shift & Punctuation (12 Lessons · Goal: 25 WPM)**
  * Lessons 54–65: Left Shift, Right Shift, Capital Letters, Punctuation.
* **Stage 5: Numbers & Symbols (8 Lessons · Goal: 25 WPM)**
  * Lessons 66–73: Number Row 1–0, Common Symbols.
* **Stage 6: Speed Gates & Fluency (10 Lessons · Goal: 40 WPM)**
  * Lessons 74–83: 30/40/50 WPM speed benchmarks and cadence drills.
* **Stage 7: Shortcuts & Chording (8 Lessons · Goal: 30 WPM)**
  * Lessons 84–91: System chords (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Alt+Tab, etc.).
* **Stage 8: Pro Accuracy & Mastery (6 Lessons · Goal: 50 WPM)**
  * Lessons 92–97: High-speed prose and final graduation trial.
